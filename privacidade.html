Privacy notice

Which data Iara keeps, why, with whom it is shared and how to see, correct or delete what is yours

Last updated on 26 October 2026 · Draft under legal review

Hello. A family’s memories say a great deal about the people who live in it. That is why this notice explains, plainly, which data Iara keeps, why it keeps it, with whom it shares it and how each person can ask to see, correct or delete what is theirs.

It follows Brasil’s General Data Protection Law (Law no. 13.709/2018, the LGPD) and the Internet Civil Framework (Law no. 12.965/2014), and complements Iara’s Terms of use.

Within each family, the family itself decides what goes into the chest. Iara keeps, protects and organises it, and does not use the memories for any other purpose.

Iara keeps only what the app needs to work and what the family chooses to put in the chest.

Iara does not ask for a tax number, address, phone number or payment details. It uses no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking tools. The only cookies are the ones that keep a person signed in securely.

4. People who appear without an account

A tree speaks of many people who will never open the app: grandparents who are gone, children, distant relatives. Iara treats their data with the same care as that of people with an account.

  • People who have passed away. The LGPD protects living people, but Iara keeps the memories of those who are gone with the same confidentiality, visible only to the family.
  • Children and teenagers. Only people over 18 have an account. When a child appears in a photo or in the tree, whoever adds them confirms that they are the mother, father or guardian, or that they have their permission (LGPD, art. 14).
  • Other living people. Whoever tags someone in a memory or creates a profile in the tree should have a reason for it within the family’s history. Anyone who recognises themselves in the chest can ask to be removed, through the contact in section 1, even without an account.

One family’s chest never appears to another family, nor in internet searches.

5. With whom the data is shared

Within the family, each person sees what their role allows (Guardian, Admin, Editor, Commenter or Viewer). Outside it, Iara relies on a few companies, and each one receives only what its service needs.

Both follow the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), a protection law at least as strict as the LGPD. The transfer outside Brasil follows art. 33 of the LGPD, with contractual clauses that oblige these companies to protect the data.

Iara only hands data to an authority with a court order or when the law requires it, and tells the person whenever the law allows.

7. Your rights

The LGPD (art. 18) guarantees every person, with or without an account, the right to ask for:

  • confirmation that Iara keeps data about them;
  • a copy of that data;
  • correction of anything incomplete or wrong;
  • unnecessary data to be anonymised, blocked or deleted;
  • portability, that is, taking the data to another service;
  • deletion of data processed with consent;
  • the list of companies the data is shared with;
  • information about what happens if they do not give consent;
  • withdrawal of consent at any time.

Much of this can be done in the app itself: correct the profile, delete a memory you sent, download the whole chest (Guardian) and close the account. For everything else, just write to contact@iara.family or use Settings › Support. The answer comes within 15 days.

If the answer is not enough, the person can turn to Brasil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

8. Security

Iara does not use passwords. Sign-in is by a one-time code sent to the email, which expires in a few minutes, or by the device’s own Face ID and fingerprint. Every connection to the app is encrypted, and the database only lets each person see the chests of the families they belong to.

No system is proof against everything. If something serious happens to the data, Iara tells the people affected and the ANPD, as the law requires (LGPD, art. 48).

Tips to protect yourself

  • Iara never asks for the sign-in code by phone, WhatsApp or email. Whoever asks is not Iara.
  • Before typing the code, check that the address in the browser bar is Iara’s.
  • In Settings, you can see the devices with Face ID or fingerprint turned on and remove a lost phone.
  • Protect the email you use on Iara with a strong password and two-step verification. Whoever gets into the email can get into the chest.
  • Be wary of links received in Iara’s name. When in doubt, open the app directly and write to support.

9. Changes and contact

When this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it. If the change is important, Iara gives notice in the app and by email before it takes effect.

For any question about privacy: contact@iara.family, or Settings › Support.
